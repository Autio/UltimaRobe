I've been building UltimaRobe: a self-hosted wardrobe that feels like an old-school RPG inventory.

Upload your actual clothes, turn them into pixel sprites, and drag them onto your character to design outfits. It includes your own avatar, two visual themes, optional fantasy item bonuses, and a local-AI realistic preview.

Version 0.3 adds bundled login, adjustable avatar fitting, a review step before replacing garment sprites, and backup/restore tools. We've tested browser login and outfit saving, plus restoring a populated test wardrobe into a fresh installation. Garment fit and realistic AI rendering still need work, and setup is aimed at people comfortable with Docker and self-hosting. I'd love help with sprite quality, accessibility, and making installation easier.

Source and setup: https://github.com/Autio/UltimaRobe
