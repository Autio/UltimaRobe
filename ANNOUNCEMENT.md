I've been building UltimaRobe: a self-hosted wardrobe that feels like an old-school RPG inventory.

Upload your actual clothes, turn them into pixel sprites, and drag them onto your character to design outfits. It includes your own avatar, two visual themes, optional fantasy item bonuses, and a local-AI realistic preview.

It's now an open-source beta built on Wardrowbe, with a setup checker and a repeatable clean-install test suite covering sign-in tokens, uploads, outfits, sprite generation, account isolation, and avatar persistence. Garment fit and realistic AI rendering still need work, and setup is aimed at people comfortable with Docker and self-hosting. I'd love help with sprite quality, accessibility, and making installation easier.

Source and setup: https://github.com/Autio/UltimaRobe
