UltimaRobe builds on Anyesh/Wardrowbe, Copyright (c) 2026 Anish S., distributed under MIT. Upstream source: https://github.com/Anyesh/wardrowbe

The source baseline immediately before the local inventory work was commit `09173df`. This distribution includes subsequent local inventory work and packaging changes. It deliberately starts a clean history instead of exporting a personal development workspace.

The Spritefy engine, inventory interface additions, and realistic-render bridge were developed for UltimaRobe with AI coding assistance.
